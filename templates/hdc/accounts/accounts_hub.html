How It Fits Together
One ledger, no duplicate entry

Every page reads and writes the same unified ledger (hdc_account_txn). Nothing is stored twice, so balances can never disagree between screens.

  1. Record — post money in / out / transfer in the CF Register. Each entry writes one document row and its double-entry ledger posting.
  2. Other modules post too — payroll, purchases, expenses, subcontract, office and owner payments all write to the same ledger automatically.
  3. Balances — Manage Accounts derives every balance from the ledger (opening + in − out). It is never stored, so it cannot drift.
  4. Review — Cash Flow shows the day-by-day report; All Entries shows every row with receipts.
  5. Close — Day Close compares the counted cash against the ledger and locks the day. A locked day rejects back-dated entries and rolls its counted closing forward as the next opening.
Cash Flow vs CF Register? The Register is where you record (it is the document: category, party, project, who and why, plus the audit trail). Cash Flow is where you read — the daily report over the whole ledger, including flows other modules posted. Record once, see it everywhere.
