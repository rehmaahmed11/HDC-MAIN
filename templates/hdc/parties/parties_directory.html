Where these parties show up

Money In / Money Out (CF Register) — a loan category (Loan Received, Loan Given, Loan Repayment, Loan Recovery) shortens its Party picker to the Loan Parties below.

HDC Tools — an external rental books its customer under External Customers automatically, and those names appear in the customer search on the rental forms.

Deactivate hides a party from new transactions without touching entries that already use it.
