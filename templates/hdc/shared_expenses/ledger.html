{# ── How the module works (read once, so nobody has to guess) ────────────── #}
How a shared expense works here
1
Record the spend

Either pay it from an account on the expense form (it posts to the Accounts section's own register engineer — hdc_account_txn), or record the payment in Accounts first and link it here.

2
Split it between heads

Tick the heads that share it — three, four or more. Equal, custom amounts or percentages; the odd paisa is handed out so the slices always re-add to the total exactly.

3
Settle up

When one head repays another, record the settlement here. It can post the matching transfer in Accounts, so the cash movement and the ledger of who-owes-whom agree.
